Total Recog keeps the interface minimal so the tension can stay human. Read or listen to Via, choose what to ask, preserve scarce resources, and uncover why every missing passenger is marked as recognized.

Voice-guided survival Via leads the player through oxygen, power, sleep, and neural stability while quietly withholding the truth.
Authored choices Small decisions carry delayed consequences across a clean, chapter-based story structure.
Mars-bound mystery The route home runs through Mars, but every clue makes survival feel less certain.
"If you wake up alone, do not ask Via where they went."

The first episode, Wake Alone, begins after a neural-link sleep cycle fails. The player wakes aboard a vast civilian Starship. The passenger area is empty. Via says Mars is the only viable path home.
